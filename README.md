# ETPumpen Skills

*Log it. See it. Pump smarter.*

Recreational skills for ETPumpen, the ETP group for people who work out. Nothing here is about
physics or computing; it is for fun.

## lift-heatmap

Your workout, lit up on a 3D muscle body. Red means worked, gray means lazy.

**You send:** your workout log, as text or app screenshots.

**You get, after every session:**

- estimated calories burned
- a rotatable 3D body colored by how much of each muscle's weekly target you hit
- a "still to train this week" list, so Friday doesn't become leg day by accident
- a weekly report on Sundays and a monthly plan check

**First time only:** it asks your bodyweight, goal, training days, other sports and female or male body (about 30 seconds), then builds the 3D body on your machine from the open BodyParts3D meshes (one download of about 560 MB, a few minutes).
